House Cleaning Checklist: How to Know What Your Home Really Needs

Use this checklist to decide between regular cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out cleaning, or add-on services.

Start with the type of cleaning

Before requesting a quote, decide whether you need regular cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, Airbnb cleaning, office cleaning, or a custom service. Each type requires different time, supplies, and attention.

Regular cleaning is best for maintenance. Deep cleaning is best for buildup and detail. Move cleaning is best when entering or leaving a property. Airbnb cleaning is best for short-term rental turnovers.

Check the size of the property

The number of bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, stairs, balconies, and extra rooms all affect the price. A larger property requires more time, more supplies, and sometimes more than one cleaner.

Bathrooms and kitchens usually take the most time because they have sinks, toilets, showers, countertops, appliances, grease, water stains, and high-touch surfaces.

Look at the current condition

The current condition is one of the biggest pricing factors. A home that is maintained weekly is different from a home with heavy dust, pet hair, kitchen grease, soap scum, or clutter.

When requesting a quote, be honest about the condition. This helps the company give a fair estimate and avoid needing to adjust the price at the appointment.

Add-ons to consider

Popular add-ons include inside refrigerator, inside oven, inside cabinets, interior windows, balcony cleaning, garage cleaning, laundry folding, bed sheet changes, pet hair removal, baseboards, and wall spot cleaning.

Add-ons should be selected before the cleaning day whenever possible. This helps the cleaning team plan the correct amount of time and avoid rushing the job.

Information to send for a quote

To get an accurate quote, send your city, property type, number of bedrooms, number of bathrooms, preferred date, type of service, current condition, pets, and any add-ons you want.

The best way to get a fast and accurate estimate is to send a few photos or a short video of the areas that need cleaning. Bright Max Cleaning can then recommend the right service for your property.

Quick tip: Photos help a lot. A 30-second video can make your quote more accurate than a long text message.
