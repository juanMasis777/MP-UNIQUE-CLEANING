Deep Cleaning in Miami: What It Includes and When You Need It

A deep cleaning is perfect when your home needs more than a regular refresh. Learn what is included, when to book it, and how to prepare your home.

What is a deep cleaning?

A deep cleaning is a more detailed service designed to remove buildup, dust, grease, and hidden dirt from areas that are often missed during routine cleaning. It is ideal for first-time clients, homes that have not been cleaned professionally in a while, or properties that need a full reset.

In Miami, many homes and apartments collect dust quickly because of humidity, open windows, beach sand, pets, and high traffic. A deep cleaning helps restore the home and makes future regular cleanings easier to maintain.

What does deep cleaning usually include?

A professional deep cleaning usually includes detailed kitchen cleaning, bathroom buildup removal, floors, baseboards, doors, handles, light switches, mirrors, windowsills, and high-touch areas. The goal is not only to make the home look clean, but to remove the dirt that builds up over time.

Popular add-ons include inside refrigerator, inside oven, inside cabinets, interior windows, balcony cleaning, detailed blinds, heavy pet hair removal, and wall spot cleaning. These extras are normally priced separately because they require more time and attention.

When should you book a deep cleaning?

You should book a deep cleaning before starting recurring cleaning service, after hosting guests, before family visits, after a party, after a busy season, or anytime the home feels difficult to maintain. It is also recommended when moving into a furnished property or after a long period without professional cleaning.

For most homes, a deep cleaning every three to six months can help keep the property in better condition, especially if you have pets, children, frequent visitors, or a busy lifestyle.

How to prepare before the appointment

Before the cleaning team arrives, remove personal items, important documents, jewelry, and anything fragile. Try to clear counters, floors, and surfaces so the cleaners can focus on cleaning instead of organizing clutter.

Sending photos or a short video before the appointment helps the company give a more accurate quote. It also helps the cleaners bring the right supplies and estimate the correct amount of time for the job.
